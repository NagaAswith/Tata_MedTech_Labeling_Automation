The MedTech Industry Problem

Medical device labeling errors cause over 40% of all FDA, EU MDR, and CDSCO medical recalls. Disjointed workflows, manual copy-pasting across multilingual packaging, non-compliant UDI barcodes, and improper ISO symbols result in costly regulatory freezes, customs quarantine, and patient safety hazards.

The MedLabel.ai Solution

MedLabel.ai provides a unified, automated, audit-ready platform that merges algorithmic UDI-DI generation (GS1 GTIN-14 check digits), instant AI regulatory scanning (FDA 21 CFR 801, EU MDR Annex I), 3-dots multi-market localization (including Hindi & Telugu), and immutable FDA 21 CFR Part 11 audit trails with cryptographic digital signatures.
